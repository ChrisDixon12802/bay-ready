# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 5, 2026**

## Introduction

Bay Ready ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

### Local Storage

- **Task Data**: Tasks, checklists, orders you create are stored locally on your device
- **Settings**: Your preferences (voice settings, wake word, theme settings)
- **Analytics Data**: Time saved, productivity metrics calculated locally
- **User Session**: Login information stored locally (email/password hashed)

### No Server Data Collection

Bay Ready is a **client-side application**. We do NOT:

- Send your data to external servers
- Track your activity
- Collect personal information
- Use analytics services
- Share data with third parties

### Microphone Access

- Voice commands require microphone access
- Audio is processed locally on your device
- No audio recordings are sent anywhere
- You control microphone permissions in browser settings

## How We Use Information

Your data is used solely to:

- Store your tasks, checklists, and orders
- Provide voice command functionality
- Display analytics and productivity metrics
- Maintain your login session
- Improve app functionality locally

## Data Storage

- **Location**: All data stored in your device's browser localStorage
- **Encryption**: Data is not encrypted by the app (browser default storage)
- **Deletion**: Clear browser data to delete all information
- **Backup**: Your data is NOT automatically backed up

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- HTTPS-only connection (when deployed)
- No third-party integrations
- Client-side processing only
- No external API calls for user data

## User Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your data (view localStorage)
- Delete your data (clear browser storage)
- Control microphone access (browser permissions)
- Use the app offline

## Third-Party Services

Bay Ready uses:

- **Web Speech API**: Browser's native voice recognition (no external service)
- **Vite**: Build tool (development only)
- **React**: Framework (development only)

We do NOT use:

- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Tracking pixels
- Third-party APIs for user data

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Updated versions will be posted on our website with a new "Last Updated" date.

## GDPR Compliance

As a local-first application:

- No data is processed outside your device
- No GDPR consent required
- You maintain complete control of your data
- No data retention by us
